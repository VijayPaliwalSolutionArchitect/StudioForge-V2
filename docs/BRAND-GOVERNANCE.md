# StudioForge V2 — Brand Governance & Multi-Modal Compliance

## 1. Automated Brand Guardrails
* **Mandatory Tone Pillars:** All copy must align with registered brand voice attributes.
* **Palette Compliance:** Visual prompts automatically append brand hex color codes (`#06b6d4`, `#3b82f6`) for chromatic consistency.
* **Two-Man Creative Review:** Paid campaigns require dual approval to prevent accidental ad spend on unreviewed creative assets.
