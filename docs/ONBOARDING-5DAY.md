# StudioForge V2 — 5-Day Creative Agency Onboarding Guide

* **Day 1:** Brand Identity Registration — Import tone pillars, hex colors, forbidden words, and ICP personas.
* **Day 2:** ComfyUI Setup — Connect headless ComfyUI runtime on `:8188` with Flux/SD checkpoints.
* **Day 3:** Campaign Provisioning — Set up organic and paid campaigns with budget caps.
* **Day 4:** Creative Briefs & Approvals — Train producers and Creative Directors on Two-Man Rule review.
* **Day 5:** Content Calendar Sync — Schedule automated publishing queues for LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and YouTube.
